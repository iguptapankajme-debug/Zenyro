    How we work

    We build for your business, not for a demo. Your data stays yours. Every system has an off switch. If something goes wrong, a person gets told before a customer notices.

    Will AI replace my staff?

    Our goal is to take repetitive tasks off your team, not to take people off your team. Most clients use the time they get back on selling, serving customers and growing, the work they hired people to do in the first place.

    Do I need a technical team?

    No. We handle the setup, connections and maintenance. Your team gets simple dashboards and plain-language instructions.

    What tools do you work with?

    Most of the common ones: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Shopify, WhatsApp Business, Slack, Notion, Stripe, Calendly and more. If your tool has an API or an export, we can usually connect it.

    Who owns what you build?

    You do. Everything is built on your accounts, and you get full access and documentation. If you ever stop working with us, everything keeps running.

    What if the AI makes a mistake?

    We add approval steps wherever a mistake would be costly, log every action, and set alerts so a person is notified when something looks wrong. We also test on your real cases before launch.
